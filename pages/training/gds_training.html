Course Details

This comprehensive GDS training program is designed to take you from beginner to professional level. You'll learn to use industry-standard Global Distribution Systems (Amadeus, Sabre, Travelport) to book flights, hotels, manage reservations, and handle complex travel itineraries. The course includes hands-on practical sessions, real-world scenarios, and industry best practices.

Global Distribution Systems (GDS) are the backbone of the travel industry, connecting travel agents, airlines, hotels, and other travel service providers worldwide. Mastering GDS platforms like Amadeus, Sabre, and Travelport is essential for anyone looking to build a successful career in travel and tourism.

"Professional GDS Training" is a comprehensive program designed for travel professionals, agents, and aspiring travel consultants looking to master Global Distribution Systems and excel in the travel and tourism industry.

Course Curriculum

Module 1: Introduction to GDS

  • Understanding Global Distribution Systems
  • Overview of Amadeus, Sabre, and Travelport
  • GDS architecture and functionality
  • Role of GDS in the travel industry
  • Setting up GDS access and credentials

Module 2: Amadeus GDS Training

  • Amadeus system navigation and commands
  • Flight booking and reservation management
  • Hotel booking and availability search
  • Car rental and other travel services
  • PNR (Passenger Name Record) creation and management
  • Fare display and pricing

Module 3: Sabre GDS Training

  • Sabre system interface and commands
  • Flight availability and booking
  • Hotel and accommodation booking
  • PNR management and modifications
  • Fare quotes and ticketing
  • Queue management and customer service

Module 4: Travelport GDS Training

  • Travelport Galileo and Apollo systems
  • Flight reservation and booking
  • Hotel and car rental services
  • PNR creation and editing
  • Fare calculation and ticketing
  • Multi-GDS comparison and best practices

Module 5: Advanced GDS Operations

  • Complex itinerary management
  • Multi-city and round-trip bookings
  • Group bookings and special requests
  • Refunds, cancellations, and modifications
  • Travel insurance and add-on services
  • Industry best practices and customer service

Note: The curriculum includes hands-on practical sessions, real-world booking scenarios, and industry-standard practices to prepare you for a successful career in travel and tourism.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need prior experience in travel industry to enroll in this course?

A: No prior experience is required. This course is designed to accommodate beginners. We start with GDS fundamentals and gradually progress to advanced booking techniques. However, having basic computer skills and an interest in travel will be beneficial.

Q: What software and tools do I need for this course?

A: You'll need a computer (Windows, macOS, or Linux) with internet connection. We'll provide access to GDS training systems (Amadeus, Sabre, Travelport) for practice. No special software installation is required as training is conducted online through web-based GDS interfaces.

Q: Will I receive a certificate upon completion?

A: Yes, upon successful completion of the course, including all practical sessions and assessments, you will receive a certificate from GTAC, which is recognized in the travel and tourism industry.

Q: What is the payment structure and are there any discounts?

A: The course fee is BDT 17,000. You can pay in two installments (BDT 8,500 each) or get a discount of BDT 3,000 by paying the full amount (BDT 14,000) at the time of admission. The first installment is due at enrollment, and the second installment is due after the first month.

Q: What happens if I miss a class?

A: We understand that students may occasionally miss classes. We provide recorded sessions and supplementary materials. You can also schedule a one-on-one session with the instructor to catch up on missed content. However, regular attendance is highly recommended for the best learning experience.

Q: Will there be job placement assistance after the course?

A: While we don't guarantee job placement, we provide career guidance, resume building assistance, and connect students with our industry partners. Many of our graduates have successfully found positions in travel agencies, airlines, and tourism companies. We also help you build practical experience during the course.
